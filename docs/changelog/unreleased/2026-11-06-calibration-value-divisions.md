## Develop

- **Brightness-dependent calibrations:** `calibrate-lightroom fit --value-divisions N[,N…] --folds K` fits hue/saturation maps with value steps (a smooth brightness-dependent part on top of the single-step correction, pinned to it at black and held from the step below at white) and keeps one only if it beats the single-step map on held-out ΔE00 across the folds and changes per stop of brightness no faster than Capture One's own rendering does, plus 25 %. Without the option the fit is unchanged.
- **Trying brightness-dependent maps by eye:** `--allow-steep` also writes candidates that fail only the steepness check, fitted on all photos, as separately named `.dcp` files in `OUTDIR/steep` (never a calibration to send); `--value-ending pin|fade|hold` (experimental; default `hold`; `hold-sat` pins near-neutral colours so bright clouds keep the single-step tint) chooses how the brightness-dependent part ends at white. A near-white check keeps candidates from moving bright near-neutral tints away from the single-step map.
- **Smoother calibrated tables (opt-in):** `calibrate-lightroom fit --table-smooth L` adds a curvature penalty across hue and saturation to every table solve, so a densely sampled cell can't keep a correction that jumps away from its neighbours' (which turned sensor noise in haze, skies and out-of-focus petals into mottled colour); `--photo-cap F` limits any one photo's share of a table cell. Without them the fit is unchanged.
